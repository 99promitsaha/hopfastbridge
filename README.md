# Hopfast

**Pay on Arc. Invoice on Arc. Swap to Arc.**

Hopfast is a USDC payments app on Arc, inspired by the simplicity of India’s UPI. Bring funds across supported networks, pay someone by username, or send a client an invoice they can pay through a link.

We want the person getting paid to think about the payment, without having to explain which token to buy or which network to use. Today, Hopfast brings swapping, payments and freelancer invoicing into one app. Next, we want swaps and bridging to happen in the background.

**[Try the beta →](https://hopfast.xyz)**

---

## Why we’re building Hopfast

In India, a small shop can put a QR code on the counter and start accepting payments. Friends pay each other using an ID. The person receiving money doesn’t have to explain the payment network each time.

We think crypto payments should feel that familiar.

A freelancer should be able to share an invoice. A friend should be able to share a username. A business should be able to display a QR code. Our goal is for each of those payments to settle in USDC, regardless of the supported chain or token the sender starts with.

## What you can do today

| Feature | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Swap and bridge** | Compare available LI.FI, Squid and Relay quotes across supported networks, then choose a route into or out of Arc. |
| **Pay an X username** | Send USDC without asking for a wallet address upfront. Share a private claim link so the recipient can verify their X account and claim to their wallet. |
| **Create a Hopfast ID** | Link your verified X account to an Arc wallet and receive a reusable ID such as `maya@hopfast`. |
| **Get paid by link or QR** | Share your personal payment link or downloadable QR code. Payers can review the linked wallet before sending USDC. |
| **Invoice your clients** | Create itemised invoices, reuse saved billing details and clients, and collect USDC through a shareable invoice link. |
| **Follow your payments** | View direct transfers and X username payments separately, track invoice status, and open transaction records. |
| **View product stats** | See completed swap activity and cumulative settled Hopfast ID payments. |

### Compare routes. Choose how your funds move.

Hopfast brings quotes from **LI.FI, Squid and Relay** into the same view. Compare the amount you’re expected to receive, estimated delivery time and route costs before approving a swap in your wallet.

Supported networks include **Arc, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Polygon, Monad and Robinhood Chain**. Available assets and routes vary by network, provider and liquidity.

### Pay a person using the identity you know.

Already have their Hopfast ID? Resolve it, check the linked wallet and send USDC directly.

Only know their X username? Create a payment and share its private claim link. The recipient verifies the intended X account and claims the USDC to a wallet they connect. If it remains unclaimed for 30 days, the sender can reclaim the remaining funds.

A Hopfast ID gives people a reusable way to pay you through an ID, a link or a QR code. QR scanning is available in the payment flow too.

### Invoice your work. Get paid in USDC.

Freelancer invoicing is part of the beta. Your connected wallet opens your invoice workspace, and a Hopfast ID lets you publish and share invoices.

- **Set up once:** save your business name, billing address and contact details independently from your client list.
- **Keep clients ready:** add, view, edit or delete saved clients, then select one when preparing an invoice.
- **Build a clear breakdown:** add line items, quantities, rates, dates, discounts, tax and notes. Invoice numbers are suggested automatically and remain editable.
- **Share one invoice link:** your client sees the breakdown, total and invoice QR code, then connects a wallet to pay USDC on Arc.
- **Follow it through:** track the invoice from draft to funded, release the payment to your wallet and download a receipt after settlement.

Published invoices keep their original details, even if you later update a saved client. Unpaid invoices can be cancelled and permanently deleted once cancellation is complete.

## What’s next

We’re building toward a payment experience where Hopfast handles the supported token swaps and bridging behind the scenes, while the recipient collects USDC on Arc.

The next areas we want to build are:

- **Payments from Telegram contacts**, alongside X usernames and Hopfast IDs.
- **An open-source payment API** so developers can bring USDC collection into their own products.
- **Merchant payment tools** built around checkout links, QR codes and sales records.
- **A simpler mobile experience** that brings scanning, sending and receiving closer together.
- **A points programme** that recognises real use and helpful referrals.

These are planned additions. Availability and release order may change as we learn from the people using the beta.

## About the beta

You approve transactions in your own wallet. Direct Hopfast ID payments go wallet to wallet; X username payments and invoices use escrow. Funded invoices are released by the invoicer, and Hopfast’s administrator can recover invoice funds still held in escrow.

Published invoice details are visible to anyone with the invoice link. Payment and claim links should be shared with the intended person. Review the recipient, payment details and costs before confirming.
